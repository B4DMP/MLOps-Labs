Possible pitch debate design changes

Currently the pitch debate interactions have nothing to do with the action card -> change that

Possible Gameplay Loop that introduces addendums: 

1) Player (PM) proposes his action card
2) Stakeholder raise objections based on their intel items (deterministically, randomized!). There can be 3 objection types using intel items
    2a) based on intel items that are discovered by the player and part of the action card
        -> the player can play the intel based dialogue option to completely resolve the objection (=remind the stakeholder : "thats exactly what we are proposing")
    2b) based on intel items that are discovered by the player and NOT part of the action card
        -> the player can decide to add them to the action card as an addendum
    2c) based on intel items that are not discovered by the player
        -> the player cannot resolve these objections. Maybe they get an addendum and discover the intel item when a stakeholder vetos

Resolve challenges using corporate noise

personal frictions or negotiable preference-based objections can be resolved by chosing the correct corporate noise (=Convincing the stakeholder). Hard Constraints cannot be   resolved this way, however the correct corporate noise can lighten the mood of a stakeholder and thus slightly increase their buy in.

Buy in
- should be mostly based on their power*objections, emotions should have only a small impact
